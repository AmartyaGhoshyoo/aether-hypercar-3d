Inside the car

Take it apart.

  1. Body shellCarbon panels carrying the livery — light, and easy to swap after a hit.
  2. Roll cageA steel safety cell welded inside the shell that keeps the driver's space intact in a crash.
  3. CockpitOne seat, a race wheel and a dash display — everything the driver needs, nothing else.
  4. Engine & gearboxThe turbocharged four-cylinder and its sequential gearbox, set low for balance.
  5. WheelsTyre, rim and brake — slicks for grip, forged rims to save weight, big brakes to stop.
  6. Splitter & aeroThe splitter and floor pull the car down onto the track at speed.
