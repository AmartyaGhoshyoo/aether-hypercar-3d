How it's made

Shaped in the dark. Built by machines.

01 · Design

Sculpted under one light

Every surface was judged in a black room with a single strip light — if a reflection breaks, the panel is redone.

02 · Assembly

412 robots, 31 people

Robots weld and bond the carbon tub to 0.1 mm. People do what robots can't: the paint check, the stitching, the first drive.

03 · Structure

Body-in-white: 182 kg

The whole structure weighs less than two passengers, and the battery is part of it — the floor is the pack.

04 · Road

Quiet in town. Not on the Ring.

Comfort mode hums at 31 dB. Track mode drops it 20 mm, wakes the wing and opens the sound of the motors.
